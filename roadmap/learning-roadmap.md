# My Software Developer Learning Roadmap 🚀

## 🎯 Main Goal

Become job-ready for entry-level software development roles by building strong fundamentals, solving problems independently, and completing practical projects.

**Rule:** I will prioritize skills required by my target jobs instead of constantly switching technologies.

## Weekly Review

At the end of each week, record:

1. What I completed
2. What I can do independently now
3. Which concepts still confuse me
4. What I built or improved
5. My three priorities for next week

## My Learning Rules

- Practice more than I passively watch tutorials.
- Do not copy full project solutions without understanding them.
- Learn concepts when they are relevant to my current work.
- Mark a topic complete only when I can demonstrate the skill.
- Keep a record of mistakes and revisit them.
- Prioritize job-relevant skills and apply when ready enough.

**My objective is not to finish a roadmap. My objective is to become capable of building software and solving real problems.**
